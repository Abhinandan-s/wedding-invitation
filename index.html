Hospitality & Information

Guest Concierge & Destination Guide

Everything you need to know for a seamless and comfortable celebration at Jim Corbett

February Climate

Daytimes are warm, sunny & pleasant (20°C - 24°C). However, temperatures drop sharply after sunset to 8°C - 12°C.

Tip: Carry a warm shawl, pashmina, stole, or velvet jacket for the open-air Sangeet evening.

Footwear Advice

Ceremonies and dining are hosted across open lush garden lawns, cobblestone pathways, and pool courtyards.

Recommended: Block heels, wedges, or traditional embellished juttis/loafers over stilettos to avoid sinking into the grass.

Travel & Shuttles

Ramnagar Railway Station (RMR) is ~18 km away (approx. 25 mins). Dedicated guest pick-up shuttles will operate on both celebration days.

Coordination: Share your train/flight arrival time with our travel desk for smooth transfers.
